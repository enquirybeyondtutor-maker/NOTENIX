Shared platform

Every course

Every course below gets all of this.

Tests

  • Timed tests that auto-submit: the clock starts at "Begin exam" and keeps time on the server. Tutors can reset the timer or leave an attempt out of the stats.
  • Question types: multiple choice, typed numeric answers, and written answers that a tutor marks. Students can answer written questions with photos or an attached PDF.
  • Tutors make tests with AI, from a PDF, from photos or typed in. They share a join link or assign the test directly.
  • Get your past paper marked: the student picks a board paper, uploads their answers and a tutor marks it against the mark scheme.
  • Request a test: the student asks for a test on a topic and the team builds and assigns it (free for now).
  • Staff can download PDF performance reports, split by section.

Learning

  • Practice library, quizzes, progress page and leaderboard.
  • Class notes students can only view, as watermarked pages (no download).
  • Homework in one place: worksheets with hand-in and marking, plus a "From your tutor" panel and New badges.
  • Revision papers: free page-1 preview, then locked pages.

Accounts and payments

  • Per-course access: students open only the courses they hold. Work a tutor sends them is never locked.
  • Beyond Tutors accounts (name@beyondtutors.com) with parent activation by email code and terms acceptance.
  • Pricing in 9 currencies. Payment by bank transfer or Wise, and admin confirms it.
  • One design for all emails. Mail goes to the parent's or student's real inbox. Homework-update emails.
  • Sync with BT Sessions (assign tests, upload homework, see hand-ins).
BT students £49/yr per course (or local equivalent) · others £149/yr per course · 7-day trial

SAT

Own product · live
  • Free public diagnostic (14 Reading & Writing + 12 Math, 34 min) with a band score. It saves progress if the student leaves.
  • Student signs up with their own email (code check), which starts a 7-day trial.
  • Adaptive Digital SAT mocks with a Bluebook-style runner: Desmos, reference sheet, mark for review, cross-out, grid-in answers, review page, break before Math, autosave.
  • Score report with section estimates (a strong module 1 routes to a hard module 2).
  • Skill drills: 30 skills × 6 questions, plus a skill map (strong / building / weak / untried).
  • Study plan up to the test date: this week's tasks, a mock about every 2 weeks.
  • Monday weekly email to the student and parent.
  • Public skill pages for SEO.

KS3 (Years 7–9)

Own product · live
  • Free 1-week trial, signed up by the parent on /ks3 (one per parent email, @notenix.com ID).
  • Maths, Science and English. Baseline test on day 1.
  • 27 tests: Year 7 half-term tests 1–6 for each subject, plus Year 8 and Year 9 baselines. 22 topic notes.
  • Homework help: the student sends a photo and a tutor replies (5 during the trial, 30 a month when paid).
  • Trial emails on day 0, 3, 5 and 7 (the last one is a progress report). Then a monthly parent report.
  • Indicator of the GCSE grade the student is on track for. Half-term tests are assigned automatically, and the year group moves up each September.
  • 22 public topic pages and 4 parent guides (the Year 7 dip, parents' evening and more).

Kent grammar (Year 7, Sept 2027)

Landing page on KS3
  • Landing page aimed at children starting a Kent grammar school.
  • Uses the KS3 product underneath: grammar-ready baseline, a grammar-level benchmark and homework help.

TMUA

Shared platform
  • Course key tmua with its own exam page: format, sample questions, FAQ.
  • Mocks set by tutors, e.g. TMUA Algebra Mock 1 (20 Q, 75 min), with sections like "[Paper 2 · Q4]" so reports split by section.
  • Staff PDF performance reports.
  • Marked past papers from the official UAT-UK PDFs.

The exam page promises "standard and hard Paper 1 and Paper 2 mocks" and "timing analytics per question". There is no dedicated TMUA mock set, adaptive engine, score estimate (1.0–9.0) or study plan like SAT has.

ESAT & TARA

Shared platform
  • Course keys esat and tara. ESAT has its own exam page.
  • Tests set by tutors, past papers marked from UAT-UK PDFs, and staff reports.
  • TARA: the Writing task fits the tutor-marked written tests.

No dedicated mocks, module-by-module scoring or skill tracking. TARA has no public exam page yet.

GCSE · IGCSE · A-Level

Shared platform
  • One course per subject (Maths, Further Maths, sciences, humanities and more), with exam pages for GCSE Maths, A-Level Maths and IGCSE.
  • Tests made with AI by board (AQA, Edexcel, OCR, Cambridge, WJEC, CCEA). Calculator advice in the copy is board-specific.
  • Revision topic pages with a £149 checkout (sample: AQA GCSE Physics momentum notes).
  • Past papers marked against the mark scheme (AQA PDFs allowed).

Ask a Tutor (Singapore Sec 1 – JC 2)

Own product · live
  • Photo doubt help with subjects set by level (E-Math, A-Math, the sciences, JC H2 subjects).
  • Free trial: 5 questions in 7 days. Parent signs up with an email code.
  • Plans: Doubt S$39 for 30 days (20 questions), Plus S$79 (50 questions + 2 live calls), annual S$249.
  • Welcome, "trial ending" and renewal emails. Payment to an SGD account.

Not in the sitemap yet. Prices in other currencies are still placeholders.

Biggest gaps

  • TMUA / ESAT / TARA are the main admissions products but have no engine of their own. A SAT-style build (mock sets, score estimate, skill map, plan, weekly email) is the obvious next step.
  • AU/NZ maths courses now exist, but they need content: at least a diagnostic for each course.
  • Exam-page promises on TMUA and others should either be built or toned down.
  • Uncommitted work: KS3 changes, SAT mock 3, sitemap.

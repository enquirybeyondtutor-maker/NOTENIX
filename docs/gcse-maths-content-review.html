gcse-maths-diagnostic-non-calc-01 · Number · Fractions, decimals and percentages · difficulty 1
Work out $\dfrac{3}{4} - \dfrac{2}{5}$.
  1. $\dfrac{1}{20}$
  2. $\dfrac{7}{20}$
  3. $\dfrac{1}{9}$
  4. $\dfrac{5}{9}$
Answer B. Use a common denominator of $20$: $\frac{15}{20} - \frac{8}{20} = \frac{7}{20}$.
gcse-maths-diagnostic-non-calc-02 · Number · Indices, standard form and surds · difficulty 2
Work out the value of $27^{\frac{2}{3}}$.
  1. $18$
  2. $6$
  3. $81$
  4. $9$
Answer D. The denominator $3$ means cube root and the numerator $2$ means square: $\sqrt[3]{27} = 3$ and $3^2 = 9$.
gcse-maths-diagnostic-non-calc-03 · Number · Factors, multiples and estimation · difficulty 1
What is the highest common factor (HCF) of $84$ and $126$?
  1. $42$
  2. $21$
  3. $14$
  4. $6$
Answer A. $84 = 2^2 \times 3 \times 7$ and $126 = 2 \times 3^2 \times 7$. The shared factors are $2 \times 3 \times 7 = 42$.
gcse-maths-diagnostic-non-calc-04 · Algebra · Expressions and formulae · difficulty 2
Expand and simplify $(2x - 3)(x + 4)$.
  1. $2x^2 - 12$
  2. $2x^2 + 11x - 12$
  3. $2x^2 + 5x - 12$
  4. $2x^2 - 5x - 12$
Answer C. Multiply each term: $2x^2 + 8x - 3x - 12$. Collect the $x$ terms: $2x^2 + 5x - 12$.
gcse-maths-diagnostic-non-calc-05 · Algebra · Equations and inequalities · difficulty 2
Solve $5x - 7 = 2x + 11$.
  1. $x = 6$
  2. $x = 18$
  3. $x = \dfrac{4}{3}$
  4. $x = \dfrac{18}{7}$
Answer A. Subtract $2x$ from both sides: $3x - 7 = 11$. Add $7$: $3x = 18$, so $x = 6$.
gcse-maths-diagnostic-non-calc-06 · Algebra · Sequences · difficulty 2
The first four terms of a sequence are $7,\ 11,\ 15,\ 19$. What is the $n$th term?
  1. $n + 4$
  2. $4n - 3$
  3. $3n + 4$
  4. $4n + 3$
Answer D. The terms go up by $4$ each time, so the rule starts $4n$. When $n = 1$, $4n = 4$, and the first term is $7$, so add $3$: $4n + 3$.
gcse-maths-diagnostic-non-calc-07 · Ratio and proportion · Ratio and proportion · difficulty 1
£120 is shared between Amy and Ben in the ratio $3 : 5$. How much does Ben, who has the larger share, receive?
  1. £45
  2. £75
  3. £72
  4. £80
Answer B. There are $3 + 5 = 8$ parts, so one part is £$120 \div 8$ = £15. Ben has $5$ parts: $5 \times 15$ = £75.
gcse-maths-diagnostic-non-calc-08 · Geometry and measures · Angles and shapes · difficulty 2
Each interior angle of a regular polygon is $150^\circ$. How many sides does the polygon have?
  1. $10$
  2. $15$
  3. $12$
  4. $6$
Answer C. Each exterior angle is $180^\circ - 150^\circ = 30^\circ$. Exterior angles add up to $360^\circ$, so there are $360 \div 30 = 12$ sides.
gcse-maths-diagnostic-non-calc-09 · Number · Indices, standard form and surds · difficulty 3
Simplify $\sqrt{75} - \sqrt{12}$.
  1. $3\sqrt{3}$
  2. $\sqrt{63}$
  3. $7\sqrt{3}$
  4. $3\sqrt{7}$
Answer A. $\sqrt{75} = \sqrt{25 \times 3} = 5\sqrt{3}$ and $\sqrt{12} = \sqrt{4 \times 3} = 2\sqrt{3}$. So the answer is $5\sqrt{3} - 2\sqrt{3} = 3\sqrt{3}$.
gcse-maths-diagnostic-non-calc-10 · Probability and statistics · Probability · difficulty 2
A bag contains $5$ red counters and $3$ blue counters. Two counters are taken at random without replacement. What is the probability that both are red?
  1. $\dfrac{25}{64}$
  2. $\dfrac{5}{16}$
  3. $\dfrac{9}{14}$
  4. $\dfrac{5}{14}$
Answer D. The first is red with probability $\frac{5}{8}$. That leaves $4$ red out of $7$, so the second is red with probability $\frac{4}{7}$. Multiply: $\frac{5}{8} \times \frac{4}{7} = \frac{20}{56} = \frac{5}{14}$.

Part 2: calculator allowed · 10 questions · 15 min

gcse-maths-diagnostic-calc-01 · Algebra · Graphs and functions · difficulty 2
A straight line passes through the points $(1, 3)$ and $(5, 11)$. What is the gradient of the line?
  1. $4$
  2. $\dfrac{1}{2}$
  3. $2$
  4. $8$
Answer C. Gradient is change in $y$ divided by change in $x$: $\frac{11 - 3}{5 - 1} = \frac{8}{4} = 2$.
gcse-maths-diagnostic-calc-02 · Ratio and proportion · Percentage change and growth · difficulty 2
£2400 is invested at $3\%$ compound interest per year. What is the investment worth after $4$ years, to the nearest pound?
  1. £2701
  2. £2688
  3. £2622
  4. £2712
Answer A. Each year the amount is multiplied by $1.03$. After $4$ years: $2400 \times 1.03^4 = 2701.22\ldots$, which is £2701 to the nearest pound.
gcse-maths-diagnostic-calc-03 · Geometry and measures · Area, volume and Pythagoras · difficulty 2
A cylinder has radius $4\ \text{cm}$ and height $10\ \text{cm}$. What is its volume, to the nearest $\text{cm}^3$?
  1. $251\ \text{cm}^3$
  2. $503\ \text{cm}^3$
  3. $126\ \text{cm}^3$
  4. $1005\ \text{cm}^3$
Answer B. Volume of a cylinder is $\pi r^2 h = \pi \times 4^2 \times 10 = 160\pi = 502.65\ldots$, which rounds to $503\ \text{cm}^3$.
gcse-maths-diagnostic-calc-04 · Geometry and measures · Trigonometry · difficulty 2
In a right-angled triangle the hypotenuse is $12\ \text{cm}$ and one angle is $35^\circ$. What is the length of the side opposite the $35^\circ$ angle, to 3 significant figures?
  1. $9.83\ \text{cm}$
  2. $8.40\ \text{cm}$
  3. $20.9\ \text{cm}$
  4. $6.88\ \text{cm}$
Answer D. Opposite and hypotenuse means sine: $\sin 35^\circ = \frac{\text{opp}}{12}$, so $\text{opp} = 12 \sin 35^\circ = 6.88\ \text{cm}$.
gcse-maths-diagnostic-calc-05 · Geometry and measures · Vectors and transformations · difficulty 2
$\mathbf{a} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}$ and $\mathbf{b} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}$. Work out $2\mathbf{a} - \mathbf{b}$.
  1. $\begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$
  2. $\begin{pmatrix} 7 \\ -8 \end{pmatrix}$
  3. $\begin{pmatrix} 7 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$
  4. $\begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ -8 \end{pmatrix}$
Answer B. $2\mathbf{a} = \begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ -4 \end{pmatrix}$. Subtract $\mathbf{b}$ from each row: $6 - (-1) = 7$ and $-4 - 4 = -8$.
gcse-maths-diagnostic-calc-06 · Probability and statistics · Statistics · difficulty 2
The mean of $20$ numbers is $6.5$. One more number is added and the mean of all $21$ numbers is $7$. What number was added?
  1. $17$
  2. $7.5$
  3. $10.5$
  4. $27$
Answer A. The $20$ numbers total $20 \times 6.5 = 130$. The $21$ numbers total $21 \times 7 = 147$. The new number is $147 - 130 = 17$.
gcse-maths-diagnostic-calc-07 · Algebra · Equations and inequalities · difficulty 3
Solve $x^2 - 5x - 14 = 0$.
  1. $x = 2$ or $x = -7$
  2. $x = 14$ or $x = -1$
  3. $x = -7$ or $x = -2$
  4. $x = 7$ or $x = -2$
Answer D. Find two numbers that multiply to $-14$ and add to $-5$: these are $-7$ and $2$. So $(x - 7)(x + 2) = 0$, giving $x = 7$ or $x = -2$.
gcse-maths-diagnostic-calc-08 · Ratio and proportion · Ratio and proportion · difficulty 3
$y$ is inversely proportional to $x^2$. When $x = 2$, $y = 9$. What is the value of $y$ when $x = 6$?
  1. $27$
  2. $3$
  3. $1$
  4. $81$
Answer C. Write $y = \frac{k}{x^2}$. Using $x = 2$, $y = 9$: $k = 9 \times 4 = 36$. When $x = 6$, $y = \frac{36}{36} = 1$.
gcse-maths-diagnostic-calc-09 · Algebra · Graphs and functions · difficulty 3
$f(x) = 3x - 2$ and $g(x) = x^2$. What is the value of $fg(2)$?
  1. $16$
  2. $10$
  3. $8$
  4. $14$
Answer B. $fg(2)$ means do $g$ first, then $f$. $g(2) = 2^2 = 4$, and $f(4) = 3 \times 4 - 2 = 10$.
gcse-maths-diagnostic-calc-10 · Number · Indices, standard form and surds · difficulty 2
Work out $(3 \times 10^5) \times (4 \times 10^{-2})$. Give your answer in standard form.
  1. $1.2 \times 10^4$
  2. $12 \times 10^3$
  3. $1.2 \times 10^3$
  4. $1.2 \times 10^{-10}$
Answer A. Multiply the numbers and add the powers: $12 \times 10^3$. In standard form the first number must be between $1$ and $10$, so this is $1.2 \times 10^4$.
